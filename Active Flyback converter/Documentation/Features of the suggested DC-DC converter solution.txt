Features of the suggested DC-DC converter solution

Modular and scalable architecture to support incremental number of electrolysers.
Converts voltage from solar-grid input (400 Vdc – 1200 Vdc) to electrolyser-compatible voltage (1.9 Vdc – 2.1 Vdc), supporting currents from 5 A to 20 A.
Enables multiple electrolysers to be connected in parallel at the converter output, improving scalability and modularity.
Traditional systems typically require electrolysers to be connected in series to match the high DC bus voltage from solar grids; such design depends heavily on sunlight availability, resulting in fluctuating hydrogen production and suboptimal efficiency.
Maintains a near-constant output voltage and current, ensuring consistent hydrogen production even with varying solar input conditions.
Continuously monitors both input and output power through IoT integration, providing real-time data visualization on an online dashboard.
Allows remote control of electrolyser startup and shutdown via the same dashboard, enhancing operational flexibility and safety.
Estimates hydrogen production in real time using converter data analytics, enabling better planning and tracking of system performance.
Achieves up to 95% efficiency across most of the operating range, optimizing energy utilization and reducing operational costs.
Supports customization according to specific application requirements and compliance with relevant international standards.
Protections such as overvoltage, overcurrent, reverse polarity, and thermal shutdown, which are critical for reliability and safety.
Product shall meet the EMI-EMC standards (important for industrial and utility-scale installations).
Ability to withstand harsh environmental conditions (e.g., temperature, dust, humidity).
